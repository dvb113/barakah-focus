# Barakah Focus — Focus & Prayer Reminder

A lightweight, privacy-first browser extension (Manifest V3) that helps you protect your time by blocking distracting short-form video feeds and custom websites while providing gentle prayer time reminders.

## Features

- **Distraction Shield (Focus Mode):**
  - Hides YouTube Shorts shelves and redirects `/shorts` and Instagram `/reels` pages to a calm reminder screen.
  - Custom website blocklist stored locally in your browser.
- **Prayer Time Reminders:**
  - Fetches daily prayer times for your city using the public Aladhan API.
  - Sends a gentle browser notification when each prayer time arrives.
- **100% Privacy-First:**
  - No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. All preferences are saved locally in `chrome.storage.local`.

## Installation (Developer / Unpacked Mode)

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open Chrome, Edge, or Brave and go to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the project folder.
5. Pin **Barakah Focus** to your toolbar, enter your city & country, and toggle Focus Mode when ready to work or study.
